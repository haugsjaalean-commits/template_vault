In Obsidian, search bars usually have an ex at the end to clear the search. I want the search bar for classes to have this as well.

## Even better improvement

The search bar shouldn't be constantly visible. It should just be a looking glass that expands into a search bar when necessary. The x should clear the search, and clicking on the looking glass a second time gets rid of the search section. This is exactly how the search is implemented in the native *Bases* plugin.
