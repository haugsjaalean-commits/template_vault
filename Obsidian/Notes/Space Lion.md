## A late summer rain

I was listening to Space Lion as the rain fell from outside my bedroom window. I was reminded that I did not always have to be doing something. I was reminded about the life on this planet. It was a late summer rain a couple of hours before sunset. The light was soft and yellow. The rain was very wet in the most playful way possible. There was still much light in the sky. The clouds were more interested in causing giving rain than in taking light. They went here and there before they ended up leaving for good. 

I tried to appreciates the wholeness of the rain as much as possible. I thought about the fact that I often find myself telling myself that I need to be appreciating things to the max. This is always running through my mind. When I watched the rain I tried to forget that it was even something that needed to be appreciated. I tried to be it instead of consume it. This is hard for the modern person I think. We are very used to consuming and not very used to being.
