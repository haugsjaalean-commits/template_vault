There is no such thing as an excuse. There is only *reality*. We can all make up an infinite number of stories about why reality is the way it is. At the end of the day, it is up to the individual to decide which story they like the best. Which one feels more *true*. You see, the amount of truth we believe a story to hold is the only thing that differentiates *reasons* from *excuses*. If someone tells a story about why something is happening and the listener thinks that they are full of shit, they will say it is an excuse. If the story seems to describe accurately why une telle situation would be happening, the receiver of this story will call it an adequate reason. 

I don't think many people understand that there is actually no difference between these two things. They are both stories. Stories hold a great deal of power over us. I think this is why people feel the need to classify some stories as excuses. We all need to find little ways of giving less power to stories. But, sometimes stories deserve power. This is when calling them excuses can harm another's feelings. The way we talk about each other's stories is a massive part of how we exist together. We are not so much judging one another as we are judging one another's stories. 

Seeing things this way, it's hard not to wonder why we even bother telling stories at all. If we don't tell any story, then no one will judge us, and we will not judge ourselves.
