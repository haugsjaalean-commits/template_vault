1. solvable
2. unsolvable
3. unsolvable and undismissable (it needs to be solved before updating)


Using undismissible discrepancies, I can force the user to fill in certain field. This is my way of saying: "You have to give this information about an instance; otherwise it doesn't make sense and cannot exist.". 

## Further thoughts


I actually think that this is a bad idea. I think that it only makes sense to have solvable and unsolvable, but all discrepancies should be dismissible. When a discrepancy is unsolvable, that simply means that there is a choice that needs to be made by a human. If the human decides to ignore that choice and leave it for later is up to them, but the discrepancy will stay as a constant reminder.
