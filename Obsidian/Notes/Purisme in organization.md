The idea that we can have many different characteristics of organization (on multiple different axes) makes me feel silly for some of my previous thinking on the subject. I used to think there must be a *true* form of organization. As I view all of the different ways in which organization can be viewed, it seems completely ridiculous to think that there could be one way that is somehow more valid than the rest. Now, instead of searching tirelessly for the "solution" to organization, I want to open up the question: <[[The base definition of organization]]>
