I have realized that, when I am using Obsidian, I think more in *space* than in words. What I mean by this is that, when I want to go to a certain view  or a certain note, the name of the thing I am looking for won't come to me at all, but I will know the exact path I need to follow to get there, because I have followed this path before. I find this extremely fascinating. It would seem as thought the mind is relearning how to think. I'm can't be sure what exactly is happening here, but maybe it's kind of like learning a new language: once one learns a few words and basic syntax, they may start thinking in a foreign language. If I created a system where all notes where found by typing in their name/key words, then I would associate thoughts with words, but I did not do that; I made a system where every thought is located in a branch on a large tree. My mind navigates the tree more easily than it recalls a name.
