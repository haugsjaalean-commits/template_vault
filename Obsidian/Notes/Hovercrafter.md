I was thinking about an uncomplete drawing that I made. I think it's one of the greatest forms that I have ever created. In any case, one of my ideas was that this form could be a part of a hovercraft of sorts. This was an interesting idea to me, and I even thought of the character who would pilot it. 

In any case, I believe I have already mentioned this somewhere, but I believe that a hovercraft is probably one of the best mixtures between *prediction* and *control*. These are the two principles of movement. These are the things that add complexity and interest into systems of movement. The reason for this is because it is both bidden to the earth and sky. If it has plain like features, then its movement through the aire will be quite complexe. But it will all the same need to adapt to what's happening in terms of the geometry of the ground. Driving a car is interesting because the driver has to think about how the curves and slopes of the track will move the car. An airplane never has to predict that because the track is always consistent. The benefit is that the airplane can go anywhere, which is more complexe and more fun. A hovercraft, hover a small distance above the ground, gets the best of both worlds. My only other way of envisioning this type of situation was with a interplanetary spacecraft.  

The main effect the the ground can have on a flying object is called the [ground effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_effect_(aerodynamics)). The principle advantage of this is that it increases lifting force. I imagined a hovercraft which would rely  heavily on the ground effect to stay afloat. On top of helping the vehicle stay in the air, I imagined the ground having an exaggerated effect on it. I imagine that the specific properties of ground effect are changed based on the material. I imagine the vehicle handling differently based on what sort of material it is hovering over. On top of this, wind could be introduced to add even more complexity. 

This reminds me of the flying machines from [[Castle in the sky]]. They way they went over fields. They way they pushed off the ground. 

I can see the machines moving through the sky so clearly in my mind. I just want to bring them to life. I'll do what I can to bring them to life one day. These beautiful machines.
